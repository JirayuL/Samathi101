# Asking Claude to update the Samathi 101 website

A short guide for the Willpower Institute Foundation on how to ask Claude
(the AI assistant) to make changes to the English courses website. You do
**not** need to know any code. Describe what you want in plain words — in
English or Thai — and Claude will make the change. Gear then reviews it and
publishes it to the live site.

The site is the English meditation courses site of the foundation, planned for
**https://english.samathi101.com**. While it is being prepared you can preview
it at **https://jirayul.github.io/samathi101/**.

---

## How to ask

1. Open the project with Claude (or send your request to Gear, who will pass it on).
2. Say what you want in your own words. You don't need technical terms —
   describe what you see on the website and what you'd like instead.
3. Be specific: name the course or the tab, and give the exact new text, date,
   fee, or image. See the examples below.
4. You can ask for several changes at once, or one at a time — whichever is easier.

Claude will usually show you the change and may ask a question or two before
finishing. Nothing goes to the live website until Gear reviews and publishes
it (see **"What happens after you ask"** at the end).

---

## What you can ask to change

The tabs on the site are: **Home, Instructor Course, Children Course,
7-Day Course, 1-Day Course, Contact Us, Register.**

| You want to change… | Just ask — for example |
|---|---|
| A course's **text** | "In the 7-Day Course description, change 'surrounded by the peaceful natural environment' to …" |
| A course's **dates** | "Update the 7-Day Course dates to 16–23 December 2026." |
| A course's **fee** | "Change the 7-Day Course early fee to THB 4,200 and move the early deadline to 15 November 2026." |
| A course's **status** | "Mark the MIC Online 2027 course as closed." (Options: open, coming soon, closed — the buttons update by themselves.) |
| A **"coming soon" course** going live | "The Chiang Mai 1-Day Course now has dates: 14 March 2027. Set it to open." |
| A course **video** | "On the Instructor Course, use this YouTube video instead: [link]." |
| A course **image** | "Replace the Children Course photo with this picture." (Attach the image file.) |
| **Tab names or order** | "Rename the '1-Day Course' tab to 'One-Day Course'." |
| **Hero text or the quote** on the Home page | "Change the quote on the home page to …" |
| **Contact details** | "Update the headquarters LINE to @willpower.fls2 and the email to …" |
| **New dates added to a course** | "Add a Pattaya 1-Day date: 20 February 2027." |

If you're not sure whether something can be changed, just ask — Claude will tell
you.

## Things Claude will confirm with you first

These are decisions that belong to the foundation, so Claude will check with you
(and with Gear) before doing them, rather than changing them on its own:

- **Registration links and forms.** The Google Form links, the questions on the
  MIC Online application form, and the consent wording are set by the foundation.
  You can absolutely change them — just say so clearly, and Claude will confirm
  the exact new link or wording before making the change.
- **Anything that goes public or gets sent.** Publishing to the live site,
  sending an email, or posting anything — Claude asks first and waits for your
  "yes".
- **The look of the site** — colours and fonts. These are kept consistent on
  purpose. Claude can change them, but will flag it so the foundation can approve.

---

## What Claude will not do

To keep the site safe and to protect visitors' personal data (Thailand's PDPA),
Claude will **not**:

- Put passwords, keys, or private links to the registration spreadsheet into the
  website code.
- Add anything that sends visitors' names, emails, or form answers to Facebook,
  Google, or any other outside service.
- Collect more personal information on the forms than a course actually needs, or
  remove the consent wording.

If you ask for one of these, Claude will explain why and suggest a safe way to do
what you need.

---

## Tips for a clear request

- **Name the course or tab.** "On the 7-Day Course…" or "On the Register tab…".
- **Paste the exact new words.** For text changes, give the full new sentence so
  nothing is guessed.
- **Give exact dates and fees.** "17–24 December 2026", "THB 3,900" — exactly as
  it should appear.
- **Attach images.** For a new photo or poster, send the image file along with
  your request, and say which course it's for.
- **Phones matter.** If something looks wrong on your phone but fine on a
  computer (or the other way round), say so — Claude checks both.

---

## What happens after you ask

1. **Claude makes the change** and checks it (text, links, how it looks on phone
   and computer).
2. **Gear reviews it.** Changes go through a review before they reach the live
   site — this is a safety step, not a delay in your request.
3. **Gear publishes it.** The live site is updated by hand for now, so a change
   is approved first and then published. Until then, the live website keeps
   showing the previous version.

So after you ask, the change is prepared and reviewed; it appears on
**english.samathi101.com** once Gear publishes it. If you need something live by
a certain date, mention the date in your request.
